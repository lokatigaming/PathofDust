## Healing

For the base heal formula, overheal, and heal crits, see
[Combat](/wiki/combat#healing) - that mechanic is shared by every healer,
so it lives on the combat page. This page covers the two things specific to
healing: shields, and everything that quietly makes your healing bigger or
smaller than the base formula would suggest.

<h3 id="shields">Shields</h3>

Shielding an already-shielded target adds the new amount on top of what's
left - shield amounts stack, they don't overwrite. Duration works
differently: every shield cast resets the timer to a full fresh duration
instead of adding to what's left, so re-shielding someone with only a second
left doesn't stack more time on top of it - it just starts the clock over.
That's still worth doing even on an already-maxed shield: no extra HP gets
through, but the timer resets back to full for free.

<p class="muted">Shields are capped well above 100% of the target's own max HP - there's real room to stack multiple casts before hitting the ceiling, but it isn't unlimited.</p>

<h3 id="modifiers">What Affects Your Healing</h3>

Several buffs, debuffs, and boss auras multiply every heal up or down on top
of the base formula - worth knowing about since none of them show up as a
change to your own stat sheet:

<div class="wiki-table-wrap">

| Source | Effect |
|---|---|
| Divine elemental proc (on a landed heal) | Increases the *healer's own* future healing power - see [Combat](/wiki/combat#elemental) |
| Divine elemental proc (on a landed hit, enemy side) | Reduces the target's healing *received* |
| Fire Demon (world boss aura) | Cuts every heal fight-wide by a flat amount, applied on top of everything else |
| Cthulhu (world boss debuff) | The same stacking damage debuff Cthulhu applies also reduces healing dealt by whoever it lands on - see [Bosses](/wiki/bosses#cthulhu) |

</div>

<p class="muted">A handful of class-specific skills also add their own healing-received debuffs or bonuses (some Warrior, Paladin, Warlock, and Slayer nodes among them) - those are documented on their own node tooltips in <a href="/wiki/passives">Passives</a> rather than repeated here.</p>
