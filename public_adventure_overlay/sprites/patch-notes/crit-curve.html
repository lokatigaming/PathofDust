Crit Damage: Before vs. After the Overcrit Curve

Crit damage multiplier as crit chance climbs from 0% to 400%, for a character with 5000% crit damage (crit multiplier 50x). Before grew linearly with no ceiling — after, only your first 100% crit chance pays the full rate; everything past that follows a curve that approaches a cap instead of climbing forever.
